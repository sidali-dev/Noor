# Ornament

A section divider: two hairlines meeting at a small brass eight-point star, or at a short uppercase label.

**Consumer provides:** optional `label` (replaces the star with an `eyebrow`-style word).

- Use between groups of content on long screens, or under a hero title.
- One star ornament per screen; labelled ornaments can repeat as list section headers.
- The star is brass; the rules are `line`.
