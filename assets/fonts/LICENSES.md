# Font licences

Cormorant Garamond (`cormorant/`), Jost (`jost/`) and Amiri (`amiri/`) are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). They may be bundled, used and redistributed with this app provided the licence text and copyright notices accompany them and the fonts are not sold on their own.

- Cormorant Garamond: Copyright 2015 the Cormorant Project Authors (github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant). Licence text: `cormorant/OFL.txt`.
- Jost: Copyright 2020 The Jost Project Authors (https://github.com/indestructible-type). Licence text: `jost/OFL.txt`.

The bundled Cormorant Garamond (Medium 500, SemiBold 600) and Jost (Regular 400, Medium 500) files are static instances cut from the upstream variable fonts (google/fonts, `ofl/cormorantgaramond` and `ofl/jost`) with fontTools' `instancer` at the matching `wght` value. Neither font declares a Reserved Font Name, so the instances keep the upstream names.

Amiri and the Quran font are unchanged.
