# Badge

A small paper tag of one or two words that labels state or category.

**Consumer provides:** `children` (the word), optional `tone` (`neutral` | `night` | `brass` | `success` | `danger`) and `icon` (an `Icon` name; `success` and `danger` default to `check` and `alert`).

- `success` and `danger` always carry their icon and a word — state is never told by colour alone.
- `brass` marks an occasion (Eid, Laylat al-Qadr); one per view.
- `night` marks something happening tonight or in the dark hours.
- Keep labels short and in sentence case; the badge is not a button.
